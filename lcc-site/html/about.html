ABOUT LCC

A College for Learning, Service and Leadership

Lutheran Church College (LCC) is located in Banz, Jiwaka Province, Papua New Guinea. The College provides an educational environment designed to develop knowledge, practical capability, character and Christian service.

Teaching is organised under three faculties, each with a department that offers both Diploma and Certificate programmes. Curriculum content follows Lutheran Church and national policy; the College publishes the structure students enrol in through LCC-CMS.
