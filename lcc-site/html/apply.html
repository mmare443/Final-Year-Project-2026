ADMISSIONS

Apply to Lutheran Church College

Review the requirements, then submit your application and documents. Incomplete applications are not accepted.

Entry requirements

  • Grade 10 Certificate
  • Grade 12 Certificate
  • K30 application fee (deposit slip)
  • Letter of Interest
  • Church pastor and community leader references

Required documents

  • Letter of Interest
  • Passport-size photo (JPG or PNG)
  • Application fee deposit slip (K30)
  • Grade 10 and Grade 12 certificates
  • Reference — Church Pastor
  • Reference — Community Leader
  • Work reference (if applicable)

PDF, JPG, or PNG. 5 MB maximum per file.

Application process

  1. Choose a published programme.
  2. Complete the form and attach documents.
  3. The Registrar reviews your application.
  4. If approved, you receive enrolment access.
